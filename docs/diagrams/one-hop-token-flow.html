the three moments tokens move
1 Direct donation. You fund a fork like any project. The fork pays 10 "rent" one floor up. The other floors get nothing — not a cascade, not crumbs multiplying downward.
2 The torch (day 90). A project nobody claimed dies-slow: its parked tokens open up and patrons point their OWN tokens at a fork. Your fork just inherited a boost — because the crowd steers, not a gatekeeper.
3 The original creator wakes up. If the top project was unclaimed, every share that flowed up parked there — piling up, waiting. The day he claims, YEARS of fork success hand him everything at once. Claiming is always worth it, even late. That's the incentive that never expires.
> what this incentivizes, in one line each:
✓ fork authors — you keep 90% of what you earn; building on others is cheap
✓ original creators — claim anytime; your share has been accumulating since day one
✓ the community — abandoned work never traps tokens; they follow conviction
▸ and the fork is NOT dependent on this: funding a fork directly is always open — this is just rent + a baton, never a toll gate
