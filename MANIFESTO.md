# Compute Fund v0 — Manifesto

## The Problem Zuck Identified (But Didn't Solve)

Mark Zuckerberg's August 2026 essay "The Future Is For Everyone" proposes three principles:
1. **Individual empowerment** as source of prosperity
2. **Invention (not automation)** as primary purpose of super intelligence
3. **Balance of power** as foundation of safety

But his implementation contains a fatal contradiction — free baseline compute for everyone, with a **dynamic auction for additional compute**. The result: whoever can afford more gets more intelligence, creating a **permanent capability underclass**.

**Compute Fund v0 is the antidote.**

## Core Values

### 1. Individual Empowerment With Meaningful Access
Not toy compute — enough to run a real project, train a model, solve a problem.

### 2. Invention Over Automation
Fund creation, not displacement. The platform rewards builders and problem-solvers.

### 3. Balance of Power Through Distribution
No single entity controls who gets compute. The crowd decides.

### 4. No Permanent Underclass
Anyone with a good idea can get funded, regardless of starting capital.

### 5. Open Knowledge By Default
Projects that opt in must open-source ALL generated data, outputs, and findings to the entire platform community. The community funds the compute — the community deserves the results. This is not charity; it's the contract. You fund it, you own the knowledge.

### 6. Contribution Tiers Unlock Token Usage
Platform users — whether project creators or patrons — receive LLM token allowances based on their subscription or contribution tier. Different contribution sizes unlock different levels of token usage on the platform. A $20/mo patron gets a baseline allowance. A $100/mo patron gets more. A one-time $500 contribution gets a corresponding allotment. The community funds the platform; the platform returns tokens to its contributors.

## The v0 Product

Right now, Compute Fund v0 is a **crowdfunding platform for AI compute**:

1. **Creator posts a project** — a problem, an idea, a question
2. **Supporters fund compute credits** — $5, $50, $500
3. **Project runs on funded compute** — receipts show every API call
4. **Community sees results** — public outputs, findings, deliverables

## The Incentive Engine — How Efficiency Gets Rewarded

### Token Efficiency Incentive (CRITICAL FEATURE)

The platform's incentive model is self-regulating through radical transparency:

1. **Teams publish token estimates publicly** — "This milestone will cost 50K tokens"
2. **Community funds it** — supporters commit tokens based on the estimate
3. **Actual burn is public** — every visitor sees the real token usage
4. **Under-budget teams keep the leftover** — their reward for efficiency
5. **Over-budget teams get bad ratings** — if they fail to deliver, the community rates them down

### Why Over-Estimating Doesn't Break It

- If a team says "100K tokens" but similar projects cost 30K, the community won't fund the overpriced one
- If a copycat undercuts at "10K tokens" but never delivers, they get bad ratings and won't get funded again
- The rating system creates a track record — honest, efficient teams rise to the top
- Popular projects CAN charge more — the community willingly overfunds projects they're excited about. That's the team's margin

### Leftover Token Allocation

When a team completes a milestone under budget, the leftover tokens are theirs:
- **Stack** — roll into their next milestone to make it bigger
- **Transfer** — send to another team working on something they care about
- **External** — they earned it, they can use it outside the community

### The Sweet Spot

Teams learn to estimate with a safe margin — not too high (community won't fund), not too low (they'll run out and fail). The platform studies the data over time and suggests optimal margin percentages. The platform fee itself is voted on by the community.

### Plugin/Dependency Revenue Splits

Projects share revenue with the tools they depend on:
- Required dependencies (like ComfyUI) get the largest share — they're essential
- Optional integrations (like Obsidian) get a smaller share
- Default: required plugins get 10% each, project team gets the rest
- This incentivizes dependency maintainers to keep their tools working with the platform
- Companies are incentivized to donate to projects using their products — one cup fills the other

### Community-Driven Milestones

The community votes on which milestones get funded and activated:
- Proposed milestones get upvoted/downvoted
- High-vote milestones rise to the top and get the "Community Priority" badge
- The community decides what AI researches — not Big Tech burning billions in every direction
- This refocuses AI compute on real human needs, not metaverse experiments

### Team Ratings

After milestone completion, the community rates the team:
- 1-5 stars + optional review text
- Aggregate rating shown on team profile (average + count)
- Low-rated teams (< 2.5 avg) get "Needs Improvement" badge
- High-rated teams (>= 4.5 avg) get "Top Performer" badge
- This deters copycats and rewards competent, efficient teams
